About AI Boardroom

What is AI Boardroom?

AI Boardroom is an intelligent board management system designed to enhance governance, streamline meetings, and improve decision-making processes for boards of directors.

Key Features

  • Meeting Management: Schedule and organize board meetings efficiently
  • Agenda Planning: Create and manage detailed meeting agendas
  • Document Repository: Store and manage all board documents in one place
  • AI-Powered Summarization: Automatically generate meeting summaries and extract action items
  • Minutes Tracking: Record and maintain comprehensive meeting minutes
  • Action Items: Track decisions and follow-ups from meetings

Technology Stack

Built with Flask (Python), SQLAlchemy, and modern web technologies for a robust and scalable solution.
